Open Positions

Graduate Students M.S. / Ph.D.

Recruited on a rolling basis for spring and fall admission. Funded positions are tied to ongoing research projects.

Postdoctoral Researchers

Openings depend on active grants. A strong publication record in security, machine learning, or biomedical AI is expected.

Research Interns

Undergraduate and visiting internships for students who want hands-on research experience before committing to graduate study.

Research Developers

Engineering-focused roles building the systems, datasets, and tools behind our research projects.

What We Look For

  • A solid foundation in computer science, systems, or applied mathematics — though we welcome candidates with unconventional academic or professional backgrounds.
  • Programming proficiency — Python and a deep-learning framework such as PyTorch are a plus, and systems or security tooling experience is valued for security topics.
  • Curiosity about real-world problems in AI security, adversarial ML, medical AI, or explainable AI, and the persistence to chase them through experiments.
  • Clear written communication — research is reading, writing, and arguing with evidence as much as it is coding.

What You Can Expect

  • Active projects spanning security & adversarial ML, biomedical AI, and explainable AI, with publications at venues such as NDSS, ACM KDD, IEEE TDSC, IEEE TIFS, and Information Fusion.
  • Collaboration with academic and industry partners in Korea and internationally.
  • Mentoring toward your own first-author publications, starting from your first semester.
  • A diverse, international lab — meet the current team.

How to Apply

  1. 1

    Read about our research

    Browse our projects and recent publications and identify where your interests fit.

  2. 2

    Email us

    Reach out through the contact page. Attach your CV (with GPA and transcripts for student applicants), links to your GitHub or publications if available, and a short statement of what you want to work on.

  3. 3

    Graduate applicants: apply officially

    Also apply through SKKU's Graduate School admissions, and mention your interest in InfoLab and Prof. Abuhmed's research in your application.

If your message does not clearly explain why you are interested in InfoLab specifically, it may be overlooked. Personalized, thoughtful inquiries that reference our papers or projects are strongly encouraged — generic mass emails are not.
