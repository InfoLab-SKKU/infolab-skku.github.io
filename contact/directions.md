# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-diamond-turn-right" %}Getting to InfoLab

We are located on the **Natural Science Campus** of Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), Suwon, South Korea.

**Building:** College of Software Convergence (소프트웨어융합대학)  
**Address:** 2066 Seobu-ro, Jangan-gu, Suwon-si, Gyeonggi-do, 16419, South Korea

---

## By Subway (Seoul Metro / Bundang Line)

The most convenient route from Seoul:

1. Take the **Bundang Line (yellow)** to **Suwon Station (수원역)**.
2. From Suwon Station, take **Bus 82** or a taxi to the SKKU Natural Science Campus (\~15 min).
3. Enter through the main gate and follow signs to the College of Software Convergence.

---

## By Bus

Several express buses connect Seoul and Suwon; alight at **Suwon Intercity Bus Terminal** or **Suwon Station**, then take a local bus or taxi to campus.

---

## By Car / Taxi

- From the **Gyeongbu Expressway**, take the **Suwon IC** exit and head toward Jangan-gu.
- Visitor parking is available inside the campus main gate. Please register your vehicle at the security booth on arrival.
