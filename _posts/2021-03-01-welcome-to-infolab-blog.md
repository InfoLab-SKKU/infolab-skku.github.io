Welcome to the official InfoLab blog. This space will serve as our home for research updates, paper announcements, lab news, and occasional deep-dives into topics at the frontier of AI security and biomedical AI.

<!-- more -->

**What you can expect here:**

- **Paper announcements** -- plain-language summaries of our published research
- **Lab news** -- welcomes for new members, graduations, conference trips, and milestones
- **Research insights** -- longer-form posts on AI security, biomedical AI, and trustworthy ML
- **Open-source releases** -- announcements for new code and datasets from InfoLab

InfoLab at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU) conducts research at the intersection of **AI security**, **adversarial machine learning**, **biomedical AI**, and **explainable AI**. Visit our [Team](/team/) and [Contact](/contact/) pages to learn more.
